Aromatic vs. aliphatic

  • Aliphatic examples: Digoxin, Coniine
  • Aromatic examples: Morphine, Hydroxychloroquine
  • Biosynthesis of secondary compounds always starts from primary metabolites

The two master pathways

  • Shikimic acid pathway → aromatic secondary metabolites
  • Acetate–mevalonic acid pathway → aliphatic secondary metabolites
  • A mixed pathway can draw on both shikimate and mevalonate routes

Shikimic acid pathway — key facts

  • Named after the Japanese flower Shikimi; shikimic acid first isolated in 1885
  • Found in plants, fungi, bacteria — absent in animals
  • Aromatic amino acids are therefore essential nutrients for animals
  • Starts from carbohydrates via glycolysis (PEP) + pentose phosphate pathway (erythrose-4-phosphate)
  • Produces phenylalanine, tyrosine, tryptophan
  • Precursor of tannins, flavonoids, coumarins, alkaloids

Shikimate pathway — sequence

  • PEP + Erythrose-4-P → 2-keto-3-deoxy-7-phospho-D-glucoheptonic acid
  • Cyclization + dephosphorylation → 3-Dehydroquinic acid
  • Dehydration → 3-Dehydroshikimic acid
  • Hydrogenation (keto → OH) → Shikimic acid
  • Phosphorylation + PEP, −H₂O → Chorismic acid (key branch point)

Chorismic acid — two branches

  • Branch 1: → anthranilic acid → Tryptophan
  • Branch 2: shifting rearrangement → Prephenic acid (last non-aromatic intermediate)
  • Prephenic acid → dehydration + decarboxylation → phenylpyruvic acid → Phenylalanine
  • Prephenic acid → dehydrogenation + decarboxylation → p-hydroxyphenylpyruvic acid → Tyrosine

Acetate–mevalonate pathway — key facts

  • = Isoprenoid biosynthesis
  • Builds aliphatic actives: terpenoids and steroids
  • Basic building block: the isoprenoid (isoprene) unit
  • Starting material: Acetyl-CoA (from glycolysis/pyruvate)

Acetate–mevalonate — sequence

  • 2 Acetyl-CoA → Acetoacetyl-CoA
  • + Acetyl-CoA + H₂O (HMG-CoA synthase) → HMG-CoA
  • HMG-CoA reductase (reduction) → Mevalonic acid — the statin drug target
  • Phosphorylation + decarboxylation → IPP (isopentenyl pyrophosphate, C5, the isoprene unit)
  • IPP ⇌ DMAP (dimethylallyl pyrophosphate)
